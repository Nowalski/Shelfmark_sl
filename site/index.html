What you can do

Search, organise, and clear out.

Work from a local catalogue of your Firestorm inventory, with the details you need to review each item.

Move items to Trash

On Windows, send one selected item to Firestorm’s Trash. Shelfmark confirms the move before updating the catalogue.

Search your inventory

Search item names, descriptions, folder paths, and your tags. Browse folders or review groups of possible duplicates and packages.

Keep your own context

Add private notes, tags, and reference photos. Save useful searches and group favourite items into collections.

Candidate groups are clues to review. Importing and browsing a snapshot are read-only.

Getting started

Start with your Firestorm cache.

Install Shelfmark, choose an avatar snapshot, and build your catalogue on your computer.

Follow the full walkthrough →
  1. 01

    Import your snapshot

    Choose Import Firestorm cache and confirm the matching avatar. Close Firestorm first for the freshest saved cache.

  2. 02

    Search and organise

    Find an item by name or folder, add your own notes and tags, and save it to a collection.

  3. 03

    Connect Firestorm for Trash

    On Windows, connect the matching stock Firestorm session on the Second Life main grid, review a selected item, and move it to Trash.

Kept on your device

Your catalogue stays on your computer.

Your inventory, notes, tags, and photos are not uploaded to a Shelfmark service. Shelfmark reads the cache you select and never asks for your Second Life password.

Windows Trash actions run through stock Firestorm on your computer. The desktop app contacts GitHub Releases to check for signed updates.

What you can do

Search, organise, and clear out.

Work from a local catalogue of your Firestorm or Aurora Viewer inventory, with the details you need to review each item.

Move items to Trash

With the optional Firestorm Mini patch, move one selected item to Trash on Windows. Firestorm asks first; Shelfmark checks the live location.

Search your inventory

Search item names, descriptions, folder paths, and your tags. Browse folders or review groups of possible duplicates and packages.

Keep your own context

Add private notes, tags, and reference photos. Save useful searches and group favourite items into collections.

Candidate groups are clues to review. Importing and browsing a snapshot are read-only.

Connect your regular viewer, or use Mini for Trash moves.

Kept on your device

Your catalogue stays on your computer.

Your inventory, notes, tags, and photos are not uploaded to a Shelfmark service. Shelfmark reads the cache you select and never asks for your Second Life password.

Viewer connections run locally. Firestorm Mini is a separate experimental viewer download, not part of the Shelfmark installer. The desktop app contacts GitHub Releases to check for signed updates.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Does Shelfmark need my Second Life password?

No. Shelfmark reads the inventory snapshot your viewer already saved on your computer. It never logs in to Second Life and never asks for your password.

Where does Shelfmark find my inventory?

Viewers save an inventory snapshot in their cache folder: Firestorm as <avatar-id>.inv.llsd.gz, Aurora Viewer as an encrypted file under store/inventory. Shelfmark checks the usual cache locations and the custom cache folder set in your viewer’s preferences. Choose Browse if yours is somewhere else.

Why doesn’t my newest purchase show up?

The viewer writes its snapshot when you log out. Close the viewer, then refresh the snapshot in Shelfmark to pick up recent changes.

Does Shelfmark work with Aurora Viewer?

Yes, for importing and browsing. Aurora encrypts its cache with a key kept in your system’s credential store; Shelfmark reads that key on the same computer, only to open your inventory snapshot, and never changes it. Live connection and Trash moves are Firestorm-only. See the Aurora notes.

Can Shelfmark delete items?

Not by itself. Browsing is read-only. On Windows, the optional Firestorm Mini build can move one confirmed item at a time to Trash; nothing is permanently deleted, and you can restore it from Trash in your viewer.

Are the duplicate groups reliable?

They are clues, not verdicts. Items with similar names may be different versions, colours, or no-copy originals, so review each group before you move anything.

Something is wrong. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub, or read the full FAQ first.

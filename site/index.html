What you can do

Search, organise, and clear out.

Work from a local catalogue of your Firestorm inventory, with the details you need to review each item.

Move items to Trash

With the optional Firestorm Mini patch, move one selected item to Trash on Windows. Firestorm asks first; Shelfmark checks the live location.

Search your inventory

Search item names, descriptions, folder paths, and your tags. Browse folders or review groups of possible duplicates and packages.

Keep your own context

Add private notes, tags, and reference photos. Save useful searches and group favourite items into collections.

Candidate groups are clues to review. Importing and browsing a snapshot are read-only.

Two ways to connect

Connect your regular viewer, or use Mini for Trash moves.

Importing and browsing do not need a live viewer. These options are for connecting Shelfmark to Firestorm on Windows.

01 Standard setup

Stock Firestorm

Connect the viewer you already use. Shelfmark reads the active avatar and checks it against your selected snapshot. Packaged releases do not move items through stock Firestorm yet.

Connect stock Firestorm →

02 Experimental

Firestorm Mini

Firestorm Mini is Shelfmark’s name for an experimental Firestorm 7.2.5 build that adds confirmed one-item Trash moves. The Windows installer uses an open configuration without FMOD sound or voice. This is not an official Firestorm release.

Download the Windows viewer → Build from source and read the terms →
FIRESTORM MINI LIMITS

Windows and the Second Life main grid · one item at a time · Trash only

Every move needs confirmation in Firestorm. Shelfmark checks the live location; the bridge does not permanently delete items.

Getting started

Start with your Firestorm cache.

Install Shelfmark, choose an avatar snapshot, and build your catalogue on your computer.

Follow the full walkthrough →
  1. 01

    Import your snapshot

    Choose Import Firestorm cache and confirm the matching avatar. Close Firestorm first for the freshest saved cache.

  2. 02

    Search and organise

    Find an item by name or folder, add your own notes and tags, and save it to a collection.

  3. 03

    Choose a viewer connection

    Use stock Firestorm to check the active avatar, or install the optional Firestorm Mini viewer for confirmed Trash moves.

Kept on your device

Your catalogue stays on your computer.

Your inventory, notes, tags, and photos are not uploaded to a Shelfmark service. Shelfmark reads the cache you select and never asks for your Second Life password.

Viewer connections run locally. Firestorm Mini is a separate experimental viewer download, not part of the Shelfmark installer. The desktop app contacts GitHub Releases to check for signed updates.

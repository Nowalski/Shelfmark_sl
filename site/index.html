S

Private by default

Your inventory stays yours.

Shelfmark reads only the cache you select. Inventory data, personal notes, and reference photos stay on your device; they are not uploaded to a Shelfmark service. On Windows, Trash actions run through stock Firestorm on your computer. The desktop app contacts GitHub Releases to check for signed updates. Shelfmark does not ask for your Second Life password.
